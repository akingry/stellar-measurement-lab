Details

Matching method: exhaustive comparison with sampled isochrone points, requiring every enabled measurement to lie inside its stated ± tolerance. Cards are ordered by the smallest sum of squared residuals divided by tolerances. Scores and point counts are not probabilities. No population prior, phase-lifetime weighting, interpolation, or statistical posterior is implied. Missing matches can reflect grid spacing, coverage, model physics, inconsistent inputs, or a genuinely unusual star.

Composition: the optional input is bulk surface [M/H] = log₁₀[(Z/X)/(Z/X)reference], where X is hydrogen mass fraction and Z is the mass fraction in elements heavier than helium. Surface Z is computed from 1 − H1 − He3 − He4 in the MIST basic tables. The reference is the MIST initial solar mixture: Z = 0.0142857, X = 0.7154143. This is not interchangeable with spectroscopic [Fe/H] in stars with altered abundance ratios. Nonphysical or zero tabulated hydrogen/metal fractions are marked unavailable for this constraint, not imputed.

Model scope: single, nonrotating stars; solar-scaled initial element ratios; MIST 1.2. Rotation, binary interaction, atmosphere-model systematics, extinction errors, and measurement covariances are not fitted. No neutron-star or black-hole classification is provided. MIST’s phase 6 combines post-AGB and white-dwarf cooling, and phase 2 includes post-main-sequence/RGB evolution; finer claims are not made. The Wolf–Rayet flag may overlap main-sequence burning.

Physical assumptions: spherical star, effective temperature, intrinsic bolometric luminosity, and gravitational surface acceleration (not uncorrected effective gravity in a radiation-pressure-dominated atmosphere). The displayed intervals are conservative extrema over the entered tolerance box, not 68% or 95% confidence intervals. Radius/mass formula constants: σ = 5.670374419 × 10⁻⁸ SI, G = 6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ SI, L☉ = 3.828 × 10²⁶ W, R☉ = 6.957 × 10⁸ m, M☉ = 1.98847 × 10³⁰ kg. Gravity input is log₁₀(g / [cm s⁻²]).

Visuals: blackbody color is approximated by integrating Planck’s law against an analytic approximation to CIE 1931 color-matching functions and converting to display sRGB. Real stellar atmospheres differ. Surface texture and motion are illustrative, not hydrodynamics. Glow is compressed and is not calibrated visible flux. Radius scale is selectable and explicitly labeled. Tiny circles have a minimum visible marker size.

Resolving ambiguity: additional gravity/composition measurements can narrow matches but need not select a unique stage. Stellar oscillations can help distinguish some red-giant evolutionary phases; this version does not fit oscillations and will not pretend that they have been supplied.
