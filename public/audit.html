What caused the disagreements?

Tested improvements and limits

A fixed uncertainty scan with available recorded diagnostics makes three disagreements provisionally consistent, including one giant. The other giant lacks a complete oscillation set. This needs covariance and model-error validation before replacing production fitting.

Published spectrum-derived gravity does not fix the subgiant disagreements. It helps three other disagreements, but creates disagreements in 12 previously consistent examples. This simple replacement was therefore not deployed.

The priorities are a validated subgiant-compatible spectral likelihood and uncertainty-aware fitting of recorded stars. No observations, reference labels, membership, or production fit percentages were changed to get a match.
