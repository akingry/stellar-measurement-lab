What this tests

Each record has temperature and luminosity plus its available measured diagnostics. It opens with the smallest supported measurement set: position alone where the grid has one phase, otherwise lithium or oscillations where they give different relative fits. Other recorded measurements remain available. The reference classification never enters the fit.
